# Privacy Policy for Speedy Stars Racing

**Last updated:** October 2026

**Speedy Stars Racing** is designed as a safe, family-friendly, and kid-friendly arcade racing game. We take the privacy of children and families very seriously.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
**Speedy Stars Racing does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or sensitive user data.**
- No user accounts or registrations are required.
- No personal identifiers (names, emails, addresses, phone numbers) are requested or collected.
- No device location, microphone, camera, or sensor data is accessed or collected.
- All game progress (stars, unlocked cars, and level progress) is stored **locally on your device** using local storage and is never sent to external servers.

---

### 2. Advertising and Analytics
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** The application contains zero advertisements, banners, pop-ups, or third-party marketing SDKs.
- **No Analytics / Tracking:** We do not use third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics) to track gameplay behavior or device fingerprints.

---

### 3. In-App Purchases
- **No In-App Purchases:** All cars, tracks, and upgrades are unlocked purely through in-game racing achievements. Real money transactions are completely disabled and unavailable.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)
Because our game is designed for all ages, including children under 13:
- We comply fully with the **Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and the **Google Play Families Policy**.
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or any users.

---

### 5. Third-Party Links
The app does not contain external web links or external social network integrations during gameplay.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

---
